🔬 Tissue Derivation

STEP 1 Biological Tissue Model
Cylinder of radius R

The tissue is modelled as a cylinder with uniform metabolic heat generation Qₘ and thermal conductivity k.

STEP 2 Energy Conservation
∇ · q + Qₘ = 0

Metabolic activity generates heat inside the tissue while conduction transports heat toward the surface.

STEP 3 Fourier's Law
q = −k∇T

Heat flux is proportional to the negative temperature gradient.

STEP 4 Cylindrical Coordinate Equation
−k/r · d/dr(r dT/dr) + Qₘ = 0

Because heat moves radially through the cylindrical tissue, the radial coordinate r is used.

STEP 5 Boundary Conditions
dT/dr = 0 at r = 0
T = Tₛ at r = R

Symmetry gives zero temperature gradient at the centre. The tissue surface is maintained at Tₛ.

STEP 6 Temperature Profile
T − Tₛ = QₘR²/(4k) [1 − (r/R)²]

Temperature decreases from the centre toward the tissue surface.

FINAL RESULT Maximum Temperature
Tmax = Tₛ + QₘR²/(4k)

The maximum temperature occurs at the centre of the tissue, where r = 0.
